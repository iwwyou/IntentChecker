Reviewer 1

This article addresses an interesting and current research topic. Much of the work focuses on smart contract vulnerabilities. This article stands out from the crowd. Furthermore, the presented approach and tool may have practical applications.

The manuscript was carefully prepared, but it requires further clarification and clarification.

The "Introduction" section clearly presents the contributions. The "Background" and "Related Works" sections analyze current research. The "References" section contains relevant articles.

However, section 3 requires a more detailed description of the software architecture of the developed IntentChecker tool. Figure 1 is not sufficient. I want the authors to use the software architecture description method to present the developed software architecture and refer to the article "Software architecture description in original software publications" (https://doi.org/10.1016/j.simpa.2025.100802). The software structure, including dependencies on external libraries (Unified Modeling Language (UML) Class diagram), should be presented. Furthermore, it seems important to demonstrate the software's operation (UML Sequence Diagram). The software developed by the authors, its structure, and functionality should be highlighted. This is currently missing from the manuscript. The authors have made the source code available on GitHub, but without a precise description of the software architecture, the analysis of the framework is difficult. It may be completely incomprehensible to potential readers. This aspect requires further development and refinement.

I appreciate that the authors have included an extensive section presenting the results of their research.

Furthermore, the inclusion of a "Discussion" section in the article's structure is a positive aspect.

However, the "Conclusion" section is not sufficiently refined. The authors have numerous results and should devote more work to making this section sufficiently meaningful. In its current form, it lags in quality compared to the rest of the manuscript. This is an important element of the manuscript and should be thoroughly refined.

I recommend a major revision.
